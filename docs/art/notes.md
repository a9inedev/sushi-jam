# Art direction notes: izakaya at dusk

What changed per pass, what the captures show, and what was judged but not done. The contract is
docs/style-guide.md; every sheet here is regenerated by `node tools/art-sheet.mjs`, the captures by the smoke
test (`CAPTURE_PREFIX=before node tools/smoke.mjs` snapshots the previous look).

## Pass 1, the sushi (`src/art/plate-base.ts`, `src/art/plates.ts`, `src/render/plate.ts`)

- Plates are ellipses (rx 40, ry 36) seen from above and in front: rice white ceramic, a glaze rim in the game
  colour, a chocolate outline, a specular arc top-left, a rim light top-right, a blurred occlusion shadow.
- Every food redrawn with an outline, a wet highlight, a translucent lighter top edge and a small shadow:
  salmon with fat stripes, tuna with a lighter grain, tamago with a nori belt, cucumber as a cut roll, eggplant
  with a torch mark and calyx, shrimp with a curled tail and red stripes, wasabi with a leaf.
- Variants: VIP on a black lacquer base with a gold rim, the covered plate as a brushed steel cloche sprite, the
  double-decker on a two-tier red lacquer stand, the wasabi timer as a glowing green ring that turns red, the
  chef's special with the four-colour rim and a gold flag sprite.
- The glyph is an embossed stamp on the rim (canvas). Kitchen plates are the same sprites at r 12.
- Sheets: plates-96px / plates-30px with a variants row, before-after-plates-96px.

## Pass 2, the room (`src/art/background.ts`, `src/art/decor.ts`, `src/data/themes.ts`, `src/render/scene.ts`)

- Every restaurant rebuilt with depth: back wall with shelves and bottles under a pendant lamp, a lower wall,
  the ledge, a counter with grain and a lacquer front edge, a tatami, checker, plank or grid floor, a vignette.
- Palettes moved to the style guide table; two new palette fields (`lacquer`, `glow`, `wood`).
- The belt is a conveyor drawn on canvas: chocolate-edged steel rails with a highlight, a rubber track with a
  chevron tread that scrolls with the plates, rollers on the track at each corner, a warm lantern reflection.
- Noren on a bamboo rod with ties, overlapping indigo panels, a wave crest, a shop mark, a translucent hem.
  Lanterns with ribs, a tassel and a radial glow drawn behind them. Every decor piece outlined, lit, shadowed.
- Sheets: rooms-192px, decor-64px, and their before-after strips.

## Pass 3, the characters (`src/art/characters.ts`, `src/render/diner.ts`)

- Bodies keep their three shapes with a chocolate outline, a rim light, a specular, a blurred shadow.
- Props from the brief: Sal's cap, Tobi's headband, Tama's ribbon, Kyu's round glasses, Nasu's scarf, Ebi's
  antenna clips, Wasa's leaf. Silhouettes still differ pairwise at 40 px (tests/art.test.ts).
- Faces: pupil highlights, brows for grumpy, a tongue for happy, cheeks for chew.
- A three-quarter back view for seated guests so they face the belt; the visible eye and cheek carry the
  expression. VIP is gold trim (a base band and a pin), not a recolour. Walking diners stretch their shadow.
- Sheets: characters-96px / characters-40px with every state in both views, outfits-64px, before-after strips.

## Pass 4, the UI kit (`src/render/primitives.ts`, `src/ui/buttons.ts`, `src/ui/hud.ts`, `src/ui/overlays.ts`, `src/ui/screens.ts`)

- Cards are lacquer trays with a cream paper inset and a header strip with a wood-block texture, cast on the
  counter with a drop shadow. Geometry unchanged, so nothing inside moved.
- Buttons: lacquer (primary and toned), wood (plain), paper (opt-in); hover, pressed (darker, 2 px down, held
  for the frame after a tap) and disabled. Hit boxes never move.
- HUD: level on a paper order slip with a clip, tier as an ink stamp, coins in a lacquer bowl, streak as a
  lantern that burns brighter with the streak. Boosters on lacquer discs with paper price labels.
- Kitchen: a wooden hatch with a red noren valance, a warm light inside and a ledge for the next-up plates.
- Fail card: a spilled tray and two tipped plates behind a kitchen timer with a knob and ticks. Win card: a
  receipt with perforations and a gold PAID stamp; coins in gold. Confetti is petals and coins.
- Map: paper, a brush-stroke path (three strokes), stamp nodes with a double ring in the tier colour, cleared
  nodes inked green, the current level a breathing lantern. Shop and decor rows sit on wood shelves.
- Type: Baloo 2 900 added for display, chocolate on cream, rice white on red and indigo, no pure black; badges
  and stamps tighten letter spacing by half a pixel.

## Pass 5, motion and light (`src/render/scene.ts`, `src/ui/screens.ts`)

- The lantern glow pulses (a slow sine on `G.glowPulse`) and warms the top of nearby sprites with an overlay
  gradient from the pendant and, when owned, the two lanterns.
- A steam wisp rises from a hot plate on the belt every 2.6 s. Plates wobble on the corners. The tread scrolls.
- Screens slide up with an ease-out-back while the dim and shadow fade in, within 220 ms.
- All of it is skipped under reduce motion (`reducedMotion()`): no pulse, no steam, no wobble, no slide.

## Judged but not done

- The seven plate colours and the tier colours are untouched; they are the game's colour code.
- Layout positions are untouched: every tap target the smoke test hits is where it was.
- The header band stays a flat indigo strip: a wood-block texture there fought the HUD stamps.
- The fail card's tray is drawn behind the timer rather than tilting the whole card, so the three buttons keep
  their hit boxes.
- Rush and zen keep their own header colours (orange, teal) on the lacquer strip; recolouring them to red lost
  the mode identity.
- Text stays canvas-drawn; no font files are embedded (Baloo 2 still loads from Google Fonts, as before).
- A true seated pose from behind for the walk state was not drawn: walking diners are always seen from the
  front, since they walk toward the camera.
- The build grew from 620 KB to about 650 KB, well under the 1 MB budget; the room SVGs stay under 40 KB.
