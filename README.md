# Sushi Jam

A conveyor-belt sorting puzzle built on the "Bus Jam" loop: seat diners, match plate colours, and keep the belt from jamming.

**Play:** https://a9inedev.github.io/sushi-jam/ (or open `index.html` locally)

Single file, no build step, no dependencies. Progress and coins save in the browser's local storage.

## How it plays

- Diners wait in a packed grid, each facing one direction. Only a diner with a clear path (glowing ring) can move. Tap one and they walk to a free counter seat. Blocked diners bump into whoever is in the way.
- Plates circle the belt from the kitchen. A seated diner grabs every passing plate of their colour and shape. When their appetite hits zero they pay and leave, freeing the seat.
- The kitchen window shows the next plates coming: three on easy levels, one on Super Hard. Reading it is the whole skill.
- You lose when every seat is taken and nothing on the belt matches anyone seated. The fail card offers a rescue, a rewarded ad for a free seat, or retry.

| 61 | Frozen dessert: tap three times to crack the ice |

## Under the hood

- Levels are generated by reverse placement, so a solution order always exists and the grid can never deadlock.
- The kitchen order comes from simulating that solution with four seats, so every level is winnable.
- A noisy solver plays each level forty times; the tier badge shows the measured fail rate, not the level number. Levels 10 and 20 are hand-authored walls tuned by the same solver.
- Boosters: VIP Seat (extra seat), Takeout (serve a seated diner instantly), Send Back (return a plate to the kitchen).
- Map with a level path, decor shop, weekly leaderboard against seeded local rivals, daily bonus, streaks.
- Ads and purchases are demo placeholders. Nothing is loaded, tracked, or charged.

## Dev panel

Tap the level label five times. Skip levels, force all mechanics from level 1, toggle demo ads, export the per-level stats log as JSON, or reset progress.
